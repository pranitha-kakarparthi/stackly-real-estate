Our Genesis

Founded to Restore Pure Trust to Real Estate

Stackly was founded in Salem with a singular vision: to dismantle the opacity, unverified title risks, and arbitrary commissions that have plagued Indian property transactions for decades.

Headquartered at the landmark MMR Complex in Chinna Thirupathi, our multidisciplinary team of chartered surveyors, real estate lawyers, and technologists verify every square foot before it is published on our network. Today, we represent the region's most iconic gated enclaves and commercial landmarks.
